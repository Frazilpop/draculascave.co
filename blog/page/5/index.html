But Dontnod Entertainment are now also proof that creating an immersive and interactive story is a completely different skill to making a solid action game. Beginning with Jetpac in 1983 and ending with Banjo-Kazooie: Nuts &amp; Bolts in 2008, it moves from retro to modern and strikes an interesting balance between old and new games.
