The Wizard of Gloss is a PSX-style metroidvania game with horror elements. In this new game from Dracula's Cave you will explore a sprawling, occult waterpark full of hidden treasures and dangerous beasts.

  • Uncover the dark secrets of an interconnected satanic labyrinth.
  • Rewrite the rules of movement and combat with upgrades and powerups that continuously redefine gameplay.
  • One person's bullet hell is another's bullet heaven. Can you survive deadly encounters and unique boss battles where hundreds of bullets fill the screen?
