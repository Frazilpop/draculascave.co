Dracula's Cave is two years old

September 2, 2015 · DiscoDracula · News

It's bad when you forget your own birthday right? Last week Dracula's Cave turned two, and although I’m a little late to the party hopefully there's still time for a quick celebration.

Year two rocked and I'm extremely excited for the future of DC. Dracula's Cave is still making a game and whilst this project has taken a lot longer than expected hopefully it will be worth the wait – please look out for more info on this in the future.

In the mean time here at DC I aim to focus on quality over quantity. I know there was a point where I tried covering as many new releases as possible but alongside other commitments this isn’t something I’m able to keep up with. Then at some point I will sort out some of the older content on the site and tidy things up around here – this will eventually happen I promise.

Thank you everyone who's visiting the site and as always, please enjoy your stay at Dracula's Cave.
