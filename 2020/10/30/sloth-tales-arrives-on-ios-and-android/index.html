Sloth Tales: Available now on Steam and iOS

October 30, 2020 · DiscoDracula · Game Dev

Sloth Tales is a stand-alone platforming game set in the Lion Quest universe. Originally released on Steam it's now available on iOS too. Did I mention that it's very, very free? With no adverts or anything like that either.

So what even is Sloth Tales?

Sloth Tales is a platforming game where you help Jimmy the Sloth search for his biological father. I made it as a prologue to Lion Quest Infinity, but the idea here was to create a more traditional game in the platforming genre.

The game has an open-ended design where the levels can be played through in any order. A small amount of the content is randomised to ensure no two Sloth Tales are exactly the same.

Sloth Tales is about half an hour long (maybe longer if you don’t like to be rushed) and features classic platforming action very much rooted in the style of the original Lion Quest. But with a sloth.

Sloth Tales on Windows, Mac and Linux (Steam): https://store.steampowered.com/app/1435980

Sloth Tales on iPhone and iPad (App Store): https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/sloth-tales/id1596368020
