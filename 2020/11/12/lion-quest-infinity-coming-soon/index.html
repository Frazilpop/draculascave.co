Lion Quest Infinity – Coming Soon

November 12, 2020 · DiscoDracula · Game Dev

Jethro the Lion and friends steal a magic grandfather clock and embark on an unforgettable adventure. Are they here to save the universe, or is it simply a marketing ploy constructed as part of an elaborate book tour?

Travel through space and time to find out! Oh, and visit the real world too, where you get to work an office job.

Lion Quest Infinity takes the series into full 3D for a brand-new adventure and we've even got the trailer to prove it:

An experience you are guaranteed not to have had until you've played this game!

Wishlist the game today and please look forward to its release in 2021!

In other news the promo game supporting this release – Sloth Tales – is available now. This is a standalone 2D platformer starring your new friend Jimmy the Sloth. It's on Steam and it's very, very free!
