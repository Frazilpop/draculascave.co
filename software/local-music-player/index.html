Meet Local Music Player. He plays the music that's actually on your iPhone or iPad – shelves along the bottom, a Now Playing bar above them, the full player sliding up – in the spirit of the old Music app. No Apple Music, no streaming, no accounts, no For You.

Artists, Albums, Playlists and Songs with the right-hand letter index, a sort menu on every shelf, one search for everything, and a Now Playing screen the album's own colour floods. Dark or light, your choice of highlight colour and home screen icon.

Nothing leaves your phone. The one thing he does online is fetch artist pictures from Apple Music, and you can switch that off. Read the privacy policy.
