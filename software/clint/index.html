Meet CLInt. He's a full-screen launcher for games and videos on Windows. Designed for speed and simplicity, he's here to help.

CLInt can scan your Steam library and launch games with the press of a button. He's got a built-in video player and can remember where you stopped and what's up next.

Control him using the keyboard and mouse, a game controller, or remotely with help from his friend, Remote Helper.
